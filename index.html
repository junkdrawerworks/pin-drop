    How to play

    • Drag the ball sideways to choose where you stand.
    • Swipe up from the ball to roll. Which way you swipe is where it goes, and how fast you swipe is how hard it’s thrown.
    • On a trackpad, press and hold with one finger and flick up with another. With a mouse, hold the button down, flick up and let go.
    • Curve your swipe to hook. Let the end of it curl to the left and the ball hooks left on the dry end of the lane, the last twenty feet.
    • Aim for the pocket, just right of the head pin (just left of it if you hook to the right). Straight balls work too; a ball that hooks in strikes more often.
    • Scoring is the real thing: a strike scores 10 plus your next two balls, a spare 10 plus your next one, and a strike or spare in the tenth earns the extra balls to count it, up to three.
